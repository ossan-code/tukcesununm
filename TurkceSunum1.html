🤩 Did You Know?
01
No he, she or it!
In Turkish we say 'O' for everyone. No boys, no girls — just 'O'!
02
Super-long words!
'Türkiyemizden' = 'from our Turkey' — one giant word!
03
You already speak it!
'Yogurt' comes from Turkish 'yoğurt' 🥛 — you've been speaking Turkish!
04
Letters = their sound
Turkish is super easy to read — letters always sound the same!
